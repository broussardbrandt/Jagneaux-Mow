Turf War

Privacy Policy

Turf War is a lawn mowing tracker. It records where and how you mow so you can see your own statistics and compare them with other people using the app. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and how to get rid of it.

Last updated: September 14, 2026
Developer: Brandt Broussard
Contact: broussardbrandt@gmail.com

What the app collects

  • Location. While you are recording a cut, the app reads your phone's GPS position to map the path you mowed and to work out area, speed, and distance. Recording only happens between the moment you tap Start and the moment you finish or discard the cut. A notification is shown the whole time a cut is recording.
  • Motion sensor readings. The accelerometer is used during a cut to measure how bumpy the ground is. Readings are converted to a roughness number and are not stored raw.
  • Email address. Only if you create an account. It is used to sign you in and to let you reset your password. It is never shown to other users.
  • Information you type in. Your display name, yard names and sizes, and machine details such as brand, model, serial number, engine hours, and maintenance records.
  • An approximate location, only if you turn it on. If you enable nearby standings, the app publishes a location rounded to roughly one mile so other users can filter the leaderboard by distance. Your exact position and your mowing paths are never published. You can turn this off at any time, which removes it.

The app does not contain advertising, does not use an advertising ID, and does not include analytics or tracking software. Nothing is sold or shared with data brokers.

What other people can see

If you use the leaderboard, other users of the app can see your display name, your machine details, your cut counts, your best statistics, comments you post, and, if you turned it on, roughly how far away you are. Everything else, including your mowing paths, your yard locations and sizes, your cut history, and your email address, stays private to you.

Where it is stored

  • On your phone. Cuts, yards, and machines are saved on the device first, so the app works without a signal.
  • In your account. If you sign in, that same information is stored in a database hosted by Supabase so it follows you to another device. Only your account can read your private data.
  • Map imagery. When a map is shown, satellite image tiles are requested from Esri and the U.S. Geological Survey. Those requests reveal the map area being viewed to those providers. Tap the "Plain" button on any map to stop loading imagery.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: open Profile and tap "Erase my data and leaderboard entry." That removes your cuts, yards, machines, and leaderboard entry from your phone and from the account database.
  • Deleting your account: email broussardbrandt@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and the account and everything in it will be deleted within 30 days.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything held on the phone. If you had an account, sign in again on any device to restore it, or use the email above to have it deleted.

Children

Turf War is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, use the contact address above and it will be removed.

Security

Traffic between the app and its database is encrypted in transit. Accounts are protected by a password that is stored only in hashed form by the authentication provider. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, and this cannot be guaranteed absolutely.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
