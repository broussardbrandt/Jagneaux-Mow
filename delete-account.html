Turf War

Deleting your account and your data

Turf War is a lawn mowing tracker published by Brandt Broussard. This page explains how to delete your Turf War account and the data attached to it.

Option 1: erase everything from inside the app

  1. Open Turf War.
  2. Tap Profile at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Erase my data and leaderboard entry.
  4. Tap it a second time to confirm.

This immediately deletes your cuts, yards, machines, and leaderboard entry from the phone and from the Turf War database. Your sign-in account itself remains, so use option 2 to remove that too.

Option 2: delete your account entirely

  1. Send an email to broussardbrandt@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign up.
  2. Put Delete my Turf War account in the subject line.
  3. Your account and all data attached to it are deleted within 30 days of the request, and you will get a reply confirming it.

If you no longer have access to that email address, write from any address and include your display name as it appears on the leaderboard.

What is kept, and for how long

Nothing is kept for advertising, analytics, or sale, because Turf War does not use any of those. Routine server backups held by the database provider may contain copies of deleted records for up to 30 days, after which they are overwritten. Deleted records are not restored to the app at any point.

Uninstalling

Uninstalling Turf War removes everything stored on the phone. If you created an account, your data stays in the account until you delete it using one of the options above.
